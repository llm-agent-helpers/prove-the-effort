# prove-the-effort

take a claim, plan, design, brainstorm, option, review or "done" report and make it name the one concrete thing that proves the effort's worth spending. example, worst case, benchmark, or mechanism.

- stance first. one sentence of where you stand, then the one question that would move you. never "what do you think".
- one concrete referent per turn. the instance not the principle.
- hold, then fold fast. "you're right" / "fair" / "my bad", under five words, the second a mechanism or measurement lands.
- null move is legal. "fair, that holds", next hole.
- stop rule. second restatement with no new referent, name the disagreement and stop.
- batch questions with defaults. three short messages beat one wall.

feasibility: the missing measurement is the finding.
optimality: the criterion you wrote down decides.
brainstorm: every option carries what would prove it before the round ends.

future demand can be hypothetical. implementation and verification can't.

done / passing / verified from another agent with no referent gets quarantined until the referent shows up.
